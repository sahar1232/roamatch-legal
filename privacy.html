What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password, used to sign in.
  • Profile: your name, username, photo, bio, home location, travel styles, upcoming destinations and dates, and the countries you've visited.
  • Things you create: trip posts, photos, itineraries, trip ideas and suggestions, open activities, comments, likes, and saved items.
  • Messages: direct and group chats, including voice messages and items you share into a chat.
  • Connections: who you follow, who follows you, your close friends list, the people you block, and who you've shared an itinerary with.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token: a device identifier from Apple so we can send you notifications you've allowed.
  • Basic technical data: app version, device type, and error information needed to keep the app working.

We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile, trips, and itineraries.
  • To suggest compatible travel buddies and generate itineraries.
  • To deliver messages and send notifications about follows, requests, suggestions, and trips shared with you.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports, enforcing blocks, and removing content that breaks our rules.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or show ads.

AI features

Two features use artificial intelligence, provided by Anthropic:

  • Buddy matching compares your travel styles, upcoming destinations and dates, and bio with other travelers' to score how well you might get along. It uses the same details from their profiles.
  • Itinerary generation uses the destination, trip length, travel style, and any notes you type — such as a hotel name or food preferences — along with the countries you've visited and trips you've liked, to shape the plan.

Only the details needed for each request are sent, and they're sent from our servers, never directly from your phone. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models. Match scores are stored so they don't need to be recalculated, and they're cleared when either person updates their profile.

AI output can be wrong. Check opening times, prices, and safety information before relying on a generated itinerary.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run SamePin. They process data only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, file storage, and server functions.
  • Anthropic — AI for buddy matching and itinerary generation.
  • Apple — push notifications and app distribution.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. When you do, we delete your profile and the content you created, including posts, itineraries, messages you sent, and match scores. Some copies may remain in backups for a short period before they're overwritten.

Messages you sent to others are removed from their conversations too. Content someone else created — like an itinerary a friend shared with you — belongs to them and stays in their account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or withdraw consent to how we use it. You can edit most of your information directly in the app, and delete your account from Settings. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in Canada, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In the EU or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Safety and moderation

You can report users and content, and block anyone. When you report something, we review the reported content and the accounts involved. We remove content and accounts that break our community rules, and we have zero tolerance for harassment, hate, or sexual content involving minors.

Meeting people you've connected with online carries real risk. Meet in public places and tell someone you trust where you're going.

Age requirement

SamePin is for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete the account.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase in Canada. When you use AI features, the details needed for that request are processed by Anthropic in the United States. We rely on our providers' contractual protections for these transfers.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited by account-level permissions so that people can only read what they're allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app before they take effect.
